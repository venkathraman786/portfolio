Nhance India — Enterprise Insurance Brokerage & Claims Platform

Nhance India is a comprehensive insurance brokerage platform that facilitates collaboration between Nhance and partnered insurance companies to streamline the end-to-end policy lifecycle. The system enables the conversion of leads into clients, followed by the provisioning of tailored insurance solutions—including Employee Benefit policies (GPA, GMC, EDLI, GTLI), Non-Employee Benefit policies (asset coverage), and Retail policies (vehicle, individual health, etc.).

Nhance acts as the middleman by raising RFQs (Request for Quotes) on behalf of its clients to insurers, receiving quotes, and managing the policy binding process with transparent communication and automated commission reconciliation.
